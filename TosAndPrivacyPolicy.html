6. TRAINING SAFETY AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Training Safety

Gymnastics carries a real risk of serious injury. Flickzit shows demonstrations and drills; it does not replace a qualified coach.

  • Learn skills in order, using the progressions, and only try skills that suit your level.
  • Warm up before you train.
  • Train on a suitable soft surface, with mats where needed and enough space around you.
  • Only practise inverted and high-risk skills, such as flips, aerials, handsprings and bar skills, with a qualified coach or spotter and proper matting.
  • Anyone under 18 should train under adult supervision.
  • Stop if you feel pain, and get medical advice before training if you have an injury or a health condition.
  • The 3D demonstrations, the coach voice, counts and timers only show technique and pace. They cannot see you, check your form or tell whether a skill is safe for you.

4. HOW LONG WE KEEP YOUR DATA

Personal data provided by users will be retained only for the time necessary to fulfill the purposes in this policy:

  • Account data (email, display name, favorites, ratings, progress, notification settings and sign-in sessions with their IP address and device type): kept until you delete your account in the app (Profile → Account Actions → Delete Account), which deletes it from our database, except the sign-in security log described below.

  • Emails to us: kept as long as needed to help you.
  • Recent searches and app settings: stored on your device. Recent searches are removed when you delete the app. A few sign-in details, such as the terms you accepted and a guest's sign-in, are kept in the iOS Keychain and can remain on the device after the app is deleted.

On request, we delete the analytics and crash data we can link to your identifier before then, where our providers' tools allow it. Flickzit may retain data longer if required by legal obligations. Once the retention period expires, data will be deleted and can no longer be accessed or recovered.
